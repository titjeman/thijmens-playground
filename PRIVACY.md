# Privacy Policy — Thijmens Playground

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Thijmens Playground is a personal tool used only by its owner, Thijmen Derksen, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel.

- **What it accesses:** with the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read the upload status of those videos on the owner's channel.
- **What it stores:** the OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data from Google or YouTube is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- **Other users:** the app is not offered to other people and does not collect data from anyone else.
- **Revoking access:** access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **Google's policies:** this app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
